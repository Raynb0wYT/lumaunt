# Lumaunt: Discord Presence Your Time, Any Way.

**What is Lumaunt?**
* Lumaunt is a program that allows for you to have a custom Discord Rich Presence on your file, currently including custom Details, State, and Images.

**How does it work?**
* Lumaunt works by authorizing the Discord application to the program, then in which you then do your customization. Once you have customized and like your preview that you have, you can then push and upload your rich presence to your profile, available for everyone to see.
